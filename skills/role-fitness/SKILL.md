# Role fitness

Use one real unit of work and its review to test the responsibility. Inspect the pinned task brief, the expected output, and the checks in its direction.

- Identify the concrete work and output the role owns. Put the method and independent checks in the direction.
- Check that the brief and evidence are sufficient to do and review the work.
- Compare the judgment required with the staff model and available sessions and subagents. If the task is overscoped, identify where its stage slice should be cut.
- Look for causes in the direction or task scope when review repeatedly returns work. The pipeline reports its evidence and proposed guidance changes. The owner edits and accepts the role responsibility through the proposal review flow.

A staffing change belongs to the staffing proposal. A sharper responsibility belongs to a responsibility proposal. The person can decide between them after reviewing the evidence.
